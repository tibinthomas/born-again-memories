Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Growing Memories account and the data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to Growing Memories.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Choose whether to remove any connected backup, enter the requested confirmation text, and confirm.

Your account is scheduled for deletion and you are signed out. There is a 28-day recovery period. Signing in during that period lets you recover the account.

Request deletion without the app

Email the Growing Memories privacy contact from the same email address used for your account. Use the subject Growing Memories account deletion request and include your account email address.

Email a deletion request

Before publishing: replace privacy-contact@example.com with your monitored privacy-support email address.

What will be deleted

The deletion request covers your Growing Memories account and associated cloud data, including child profiles, memories, growth records, checklists, reminders, saved links, documents, future plans, Stories, Forum content, sharing information, and notification data.

If you request deletion of connected Google Drive or iCloud backups, those backups will also be removed where the selected provider permits it. Copies previously shared with other people or exported to another service may remain with those recipients or services.

Retention

Account data is retained during the 28-day recovery period. After that period, the deletion process should permanently remove active account data. Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or provider backup-retention schedules.
