Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Growing Memories account and the data associated with it.

Public deletion URL:
https://born-again-memories.web.app/delete-account.html

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to Growing Memories.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Choose whether to remove any connected backup, enter the requested confirmation text, and confirm.

Your account is scheduled for deletion and you are signed out. There is a 28-day recovery period. Signing in during that period lets you recover the account.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the same email address used for your Growing Memories account. Use the subject Growing Memories account deletion request and include the account email address in your message.

Email a deletion request

Privacy contact: tibinthomas@outlook.com

How we verify your identity

  1. Send the request from the email address registered to your account.
  2. We will reply to that address to confirm that you control it and may ask you to sign in or reauthenticate through the App.
  3. If you cannot access the registered email address, explain this in your request. We may ask for additional account information that can be checked against our records.

Never send us your password, authentication code, child photos, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

What will be deleted

After identity verification and the recovery period, the deletion request covers:

  • Your authentication account and account profile.
  • Child profiles, memories, milestone notes, tags and attached media stored by the App.
  • Growth records, checklists, reminders, saved links, documents and future plans.
  • Stories, Forum questions and answers associated with your account.
  • Family-sharing records, invitations, notification tokens and other account-linked cloud data.

If you request deletion of connected Google Drive or iCloud backups, those backups will also be removed where the selected provider permits it. Copies previously shared with other people or exported to another service may remain with those recipients or services.

What may be retained and why

Account data is retained during the 28-day recovery period so that you can cancel an accidental deletion. After deletion, we may retain only limited information when reasonably necessary for security, fraud and abuse prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or documenting that a deletion request was completed.

Encrypted service-provider backups may persist temporarily according to the provider’s normal backup-retention cycle. Content another person saved, quoted, or received before deletion and files exported to an external service are outside our control. Retained information is not used for advertising and is removed or anonymized when the applicable reason no longer exists.

Expected deletion timeline

  • We aim to acknowledge emailed requests within 7 calendar days.
  • The timeline begins after we have enough information to verify account ownership.
  • Verified accounts enter a 28-day recovery period. Signing in and choosing recovery during that period cancels deletion.
  • After the recovery period ends, active account data is targeted for permanent deletion within 30 days.
  • Some provider backups or legally required records may take longer for the reasons described above.
